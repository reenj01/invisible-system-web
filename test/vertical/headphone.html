How do headphones work?

1. Music As Data

Music exists as digital data on your phone, as a file made of numbers.

2. Compress & Send

The phone compresses and sends the audio data via bluetooth. Audio is compressed into codec and tiny radio pockets travels through air.

3. Decode

The headphones receive and decode the bluetooth signal. Small chip inside the headphones restores the digital audio from those radio pockets.

4. To Physical

The headphones convert digital audio into physical vibration. Digital audio -> electric signal -> speaker driver moves -> air vibrates

5. Interpret

Your ear and brain interpret the vibrations as sound. Sound waves hit your eardrum, and your brain recognizes it as music.
